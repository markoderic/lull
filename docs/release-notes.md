## [Download Lull for Mac](https://github.com/markoderic/lull/releases/latest/download/Lull.dmg)

It needs a Mac with an M1 chip or newer, on macOS 15.4 or later. That link is the only file you need. The others listed below are for developers.

1. Open **Lull.dmg** from your Downloads folder.
2. Drag Lull onto Applications.
3. Open Lull from Applications, and click **Open** when your Mac asks. A small panel appears from a new icon at the top right of your screen. Drop a GIF or video onto it, and it's your wallpaper.

There's a [step-by-step guide with pictures](https://markoderic.github.io/lull/#install) if you'd like one.

## What's new in 1.0.1

The wallpaper now starts moving almost as soon as you can see your desktop. It used to take about 2 seconds.

## What's in it

- GIFs, MP4 and MOV as your wallpaper, behind your desktop icons
- Desktop Focus and Whenever Visible, to choose when it plays
- Still or Animated for each wallpaper, with a slider to pick the exact frame
- Smooth Motion up to 120 FPS, and speed from 0.25× to 2×
- Day and Night, which changes between two wallpapers at the times you choose
- Fit to Screen, which converts any file to your screen's resolution without cropping it
- Clean Up Library, for deleting several wallpapers at once
- Launch at Login

Lull is signed and notarized by Apple, doesn't use the internet, and has no account or ads. Your own wallpaper stays as it is underneath, so quitting Lull brings it straight back.

It has been tested on one Mac with one display so far, so if something goes wrong on yours, please [report it](https://github.com/markoderic/lull/issues).
