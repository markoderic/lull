# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- The wallpaper starts moving almost as soon as you can see your desktop. It used to take about 2 seconds, because Lull often looked while a window was still leaving, then didn't look again for 2 seconds.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- GIFs, MP4 and MOV as a desktop wallpaper, prepared once and played on the video hardware
- Desktop Focus and Whenever Visible pause rules
- Still or Animated per wallpaper, with a live frame slider
- Smooth Motion up to 120 FPS, and frame limits for fast videos
- Playback speed per wallpaper, from 0.25× to 2×
- Full unload while paused, with exact-frame resume
- Day and Night, which changes between two wallpapers at the times you choose
- Fit to Screen, which converts any file to your screen's exact resolution without cropping it
- Advice on the best file size for your screen
- Library in the menu bar panel, with drag and drop
- Clean Up Library, for deleting several wallpapers at once
- Launch at Login
